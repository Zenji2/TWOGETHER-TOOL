TWOGETHER

TWOGETHER is a two-player instrument tool and game. One of you plays the sound with the keyboard, and the other chooses the pitch by moving the mouse. For the game, the mouse player moves the mouse into each circle, and the keyboard player presses the correct letter inside of it.

Each circle has a timer of 3 seconds, however, there are no timing bonuses and you can press at any point inside that 3 second window. Every circle you hit correctly adds 100 points to your score.

Move the mouse left/right to change the semitone, and up/down to change the octave. For example, the A key on the keyboard plays C4, however, you can make it play C3 if you move it down.

Choose a song and press Play when you want to start. The notes will play when you succeed and won't when you miss. You may press the Listen button to hear the song play by itself as a reference or just know what the song is before playing.
